Privacy on the DoAll website

This page covers the DoAll website. Your DoAll account and tasks are the same as in the Android app, and the DoAll privacy policy explains in full what the DoAll server stores about them, for how long, and how to delete it.

What this website keeps in your browser

  • Your sign-in session (the tokens that keep you signed in).
  • A copy of your tasks and the changes not yet sent to your account, so the site works offline.
  • Small preferences, such as the order you sort tasks in.

These are kept in your browser's local storage, not in cookies, and are never used for advertising. Signing out removes them.

What is sent to the DoAll server

When you sign in or create an account: your name, email address and password (stored only as a hash). While you use the site: your tasks, so they reach your other devices. This works exactly as in the app; see the DoAll privacy policy.

Sign in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shows its own sign-in window and tells DoAll your name and email address. Google's privacy policy applies to that window.

Advertising

The website shows ads from Google AdSense to pay for the server. (The Android app has no ads.)

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the privacy link Google adds to the page.

Ads never see your tasks: they only appear around them.

Other services the page loads

  • Google Fonts, for the typefaces. Your browser asks Google's servers for them, which shows Google your IP address.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and, like any web host, briefly keeps technical logs of requests (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service.

Children

The website is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Export all your tasks from the account menu, at any time.
  • Delete your account and every task in it, from the website or from the app.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove what it keeps there.
