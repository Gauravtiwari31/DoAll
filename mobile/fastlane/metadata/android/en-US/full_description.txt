DoAll is a to-do list with an opinion about what you should do next.

Give each task a time to do it, an optional deadline and a priority. DoAll's smart sort weighs all three, puts the most pressing task first and shows you why it ranked there.

How smart sort works
Every open task gets a score from three signals:
• Deadline pressure counts the most. It grows as a deadline gets closer, and an overdue task climbs further during its first two days late.
• Priority is strong but not absolute, so a low-priority task due in two hours outranks a high-priority task due next week.
• Schedule lifts tasks you planned to start soon and keeps started but unfinished work in view.
The highest-scoring task gets an "Up next" badge, and each task's detail screen breaks its score down. Prefer a different order? Sort by deadline, scheduled time, priority or newest first.

Plan
• Tasks with a title, notes, a date and time, an optional deadline, a priority (low, medium or high), a category and up to five tags
• Six color-coded categories: Personal, Work, Study, Health, Shopping and Other
• One-tap presets such as Tonight, Tomorrow and In 3 days, or the Android date and time pickers
• Quick views for All, Today, Upcoming, Overdue and Done, each with a live count
• Filters for priority and category, and search across titles, notes and #tags

Get it done
• Swipe right to complete a task and swipe left to delete it, with undo for both
• Live countdowns such as "Due in 3h" and "2d late", and overdue tasks that stand out
• Today's progress ring and your next task, right on the home screen
• Your numbers on the profile screen: totals, completion rate and open tasks by category
• Clear all completed tasks at once
• Light, dark or system theme

Your account and your data
DoAll needs an account and an internet connection: continue with Google, or sign up with your name, an email address and a password. Your tasks are saved to your account, so they are still there when you reinstall the app or sign in on another phone.
• No ads, no analytics and no tracking
• Your tasks are private to your account. There is no sharing between users.
• The app talks to the DoAll server over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
• Delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account) or on the web at https://doall-api-m1yy.onrender.com/account/delete. Your account and all of your tasks are removed immediately and permanently.
Privacy policy: https://doall-api-m1yy.onrender.com/privacy

Run your own server
DoAll's server is open source too. Run it yourself and point the app at it with the server button on the welcome screen.

Free and open source
DoAll is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. The source code of the app and the server is on GitHub: https://github.com/Gauravtiwari31/DoAll
