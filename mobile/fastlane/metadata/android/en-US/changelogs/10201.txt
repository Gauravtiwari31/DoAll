DoAll's first release on Google Play.
• Works offline: tasks are saved on your phone and sync in the background.
• Reminders and repeating tasks (daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly, yearly).
• Sign in with Google, or with an email and password. Forgot your password? Reset it by email.
• Delete your account and all of your tasks at any time, in the app (Profile → Delete account) or on the web.
• Read the privacy policy from inside the app.
• Export all your tasks as a file.
